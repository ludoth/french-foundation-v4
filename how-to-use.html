📖 How to use

French Foundation v4 · Complete guide

🧠
The idea

This app helps you learn French vocabulary. It shows a word or phrase — you try to recall the translation — then reveal the answer and tell the app how you did.

It uses Spaced Repetition (SRS): it tracks every card and brings hard cards back sooner, easy ones back later, so you spend time only on what you actually need to practise.

🔄
Direction

Eng → Fr — See English, recall the French. Best for building vocabulary.

Fr → Eng — See the French + its pronunciation, recall the English. Harder and more realistic.

💡 Start with Eng → Fr. Switch to Fr → Eng once a category feels comfortable.
⚡
Quick start & modes

Three one-tap buttons at the top let you jump straight in:

Quick 30 — 30 random cards from everything.
1 Topic — every card from one random category.
3 Topics — a shuffled mix of three categories.

Study mode — Flashcard (flip & rate) or Typing (type the answer; accents are optional, so cafe is accepted for café).

Level — Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced auto-select a sensible set of categories.

📚
Categories

31 categories — Numbers, Verbs, Greetings, Family, Food & Drinks, Colours, Body, Grammar, and more. Tap to select one or several, then press Start.

Each category button shows a mastered · seen · unseen count and a progress bar.

💡 Start with 2–3 categories. Add more as you master them.

Back — the solution

The back shows the French word with its article, colour-coded by gender (le · m / la · f), and the pronunciation in grey brackets below. See the Pronunciation Guide for how to read it.

Tap the card or press Solution to reveal, then rate yourself — by button or by swiping the card:

✓ Got it — you knew it (swipe right). Card advances in SRS.
✗ Again — you didn't (swipe left). Card returns this session (max 3 times).
Skip — defer without penalty. Card returns once later.

💡 Be honest. Only press Got it if you remembered before peeking.

🟠
Learning dots (SRS boxes)

Six dots below the card show which SRS box it's in:

New — seen every session
Box 3 — shown every 4 sessions
Mastered — shown every 16 sessions

✓ Got it moves the card one box forward. ✗ Again resets it to the start (re-queued up to 3 times per session).

↩️
Undo & suspend

The ↩ Undo button (top-right) reverses your last answer, restoring the card's SRS state and your session stats. One level of undo, cleared after each new card.

The 💤 button (top-left) suspends the current card, removing it from future sessions until you tap clear all on the home screen.

🔊
Audio

The 🔊 Listen button reads the French aloud so you can hear the real pronunciation.

Audio needs a French voice on your device (most have one built in). If it's silent:

iPhone / Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French → download a voice.
Android: Settings → General Management → Language → Text-to-Speech → install French.

🎨
Gender & accents

Nouns are colour-coded by gender and shown with their article (le / la). Adjectives list their feminine form in the card note (e.g. "f: grande").

→ Full details — including how to read the phonetic respelling and French accents — are in the Pronunciation Guide.

🔥
Streak

The 🔥 5 pill tracks consecutive daily study days. Even a Quick 30 counts.

💡 Consistency beats intensity — 10 minutes a day beats 2 hours once a week.
📱
Install on your phone

iPhone: Open in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android: Open in Chrome → tap menu (⋮) → Add to Home Screen.

All progress is saved locally on your device.
